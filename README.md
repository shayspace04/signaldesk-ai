# 🚨 SignalDesk AI

> **From customer complaint to AI-assisted resolution in under a minute.**

SignalDesk AI is an agentic customer support operations platform built with the **Lemma SDK**. Instead of simply answering support tickets, it automates the entire support workflow—from intake and triage to knowledge retrieval, AI-powered draft generation, human approval, and incident detection.

---

## Why SignalDesk?

Most AI support tools stop at generating replies.

SignalDesk goes further.

It understands incoming tickets, prioritizes them, searches internal documentation, drafts grounded responses, detects emerging incidents, and keeps a human in the loop before anything reaches the customer.

* Reading tickets
* Classifying priority

* Drafting replies

SignalDesk lets AI handle the repetitive work while keeping humans in control of customer communication.
